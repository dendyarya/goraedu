The Certified Welding Educator (CWE) credential is run by the American Welding Society (AWS). AWS is a private
certifying body, not a government agency, and this explainer uses only AWS's own CWE pages for facts about its own
program. It answers one practical question for welding certification: who can become a CWE, what the exams are, and how
long the credential lasts before it must be renewed? Nothing here describes a rule change. Whether a school, employer or
state requires a CWE to teach welding is outside this post.

## What is a CWE?

The FAQ on AWS's CWE program page states: "A Certified Welding Educator (CWE) is an AWS-recognized welding professional
who teaches welding principles, practices, and safety in classrooms, labs, and industry training programs." The same page says the credential is tied to two AWS documents: "A CWE certification
ensures the certificate holder meets all of the specification and certification requirements found in:" "QC5, AWS
Standard for the Certification of Welding Educators" and "B5.5:2000 Specification for the Qualification of Welding
Educators". This post did not read those two documents and relies only on AWS's web pages.

## What education does AWS ask for?

AWS's program page lists two education requirements:

- "High school diploma or equivalent (GED)"
- "Completion of a welding training program or equivalent education"

## What experience does AWS ask for?

The program page states: "Candidates must have a minimum of five (5) years of experience in a role related to welding
codes or standards," and lists the roles, in its words:

- "Production: Planning and control of welding materials, procedures, and operations."
- "Construction: Experience in welding, fabrication, and erection of weldments."
- "Inspection: Expertise in detecting and measuring weld discontinuities and verifying fabrication requirements."
- "Repair: Experience in repairing defective welds."

The program page does not say that education can replace any of the five years. AWS's CWE application page mentions
that "Candidates who are substituting work experience may upload a copy of their transcripts or diploma" but does not
say how much experience can be substituted, so check with AWS if that applies to you.

## What else goes with the application?

AWS's CWE application page lists items specific to this program. It states: "CWE applicants must complete and submit
the CWE Instructor Credentials form with this application." It also says applicants "must have and submit a copy of a
valid" "welder ID/certification card" or have passed a valid welder exam "for the welding process that the applicant
plans to teach." Work experience is confirmed on an Employment Verification form, which "must be completed by your most
recent employer."

## What are the exams?

The program page states: "To achieve CWE Certification, candidates must pass two out of three CWI exams:" These are
"Part A - Fundamentals", which AWS describes as "A 150-question computer-based test", and "Part B - Practical". The
page adds: "While the Part C - Code Book exam is not required for CWE certification, those aiming for both CWE and CWI
credentials should also take this exam." (CWI is AWS's Certified Welding Inspector credential.)

On scoring and order, AWS states: "Candidates must score at least 60% on each exam to pass." Its FAQ says: "Candidates
are required to take Part B of the exam prior to taking Part A exam." and the exam section adds: "Candidates who take
the Part B Exam will not receive the Part B Exam score until Part A of the exam is completed."

If a part is failed, the application page states: "Candidates are permitted to retake up to three (3) exams within
three (3) years of the original exam(s) date." The same page adds: "Any additional retaking of exam(s) (2nd or 3rd)
shall require documented evidence of additional training – 16 hours for a 1-part or 2-part re-exam."

## How long is the CWE valid?

AWS's program page states: "The CWE credential is valid for four years."

## How is it renewed?

The renewal section of the program page states: "To renew, you must complete the CWE renewal application and affirm two
years of activity" "in welding education or related activities, as defined in AWS QC5, during your certification
period." The FAQ on the same page puts it this way: "To renew, you must show continued teaching experience or complete
professional development" "in welding education or related technical training." The two wordings differ, and the
renewal section points to AWS QC5 for what counts, so the renewal application is the place to confirm exactly what you
must show.

## Is there a nine-year recertification?

AWS's program page states: "The CWE credential does not require recertification, only certification renewals."
