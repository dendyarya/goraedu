This explainer covers **Kentucky only**, where plumbing licenses are issued by the Department of Housing, Buildings and
Construction under the Kentucky Administrative Regulations. It answers one practical question from the regulations and
the department's own application form: what does it take to get, and keep, a journeyman plumber license in Kentucky?
These are Kentucky's rules only, nothing here describes a rule change, and nothing here applies to any other state.

## Who can qualify?

815 KAR 20:030, the regulation titled "Plumbing licenses", sets two routes in Section 3(1). An applicant shall either:

- "Have At least two (2) consecutive years of experience as an apprentice plumber; or"
- "Complete a department approved course that includes content on the practice of plumbing or the Kentucky State
  Plumbing Code and at least one (1) year of experience as an apprentice plumber."

The regulation does not list approved courses, and the pages read for this post do not name any.

## How is the experience shown on the application?

Item 6 of Form PLB-2, the Application for License as a Journeyman Plumber (April 2023), says to "Attach W-2 Forms, an
affidavit signed by a Kentucky-licensed master plumber employing you, or a copy of a plumbing license issued by another
state" to verify "that you have completed two (2) consecutive years of experience in the plumbing trade." It also asks
for "your last three employers" and a color photograph "taken within the last six months".

The form, as read, does not explain how to document the course route. Ask the department if that is your route.

## What does the application need besides experience?

Section 3(2) of 815 KAR 20:030 says an applicant shall submit "A completed Application for License as a Journeyman
Plumber, Form PLB-2", "Proof of successfully completing the Kentucky journeyman plumber examination with a passing score
of a minimum of seventy-five (75) percent obtained for each portion of the examination", and the license fee.

## What is on the exam?

Section 1(2) states that all plumbing license examinations include "Written questions pertaining to basic principles of
plumbing, KRS Chapter 318, 815 KAR Chapter 2, and 815 KAR Chapter 20". It also describes a drawing task: "Inserting the
proper pipe size on a prepared drawing that indicates all stacks, wastes, and vents and the connected plumbing
fixtures."

For journeyman applicants only, it adds: "The journeyman plumber's license examination shall include a practical
section", in which the applicant shows the ability to install plumbing through activities "such as making proper connections of various plumbing materials."

## When is the exam held, and what if I fail a part?

Section 1(3) says regular examinations for master and journeyman plumber licenses "shall be conducted yearly" "during
the months of February, May, August, and November."

Section 1(4) covers retakes "Within one (1) year from the date of the applicant's first notice of examination". A
journeyman applicant "who passes the written portion, the drawing portion, or the practical portion of the examination,
but not all portions, may apply to retake only the portion failed." It adds: "An applicant shall pay
the full examination fee for a retake of any portion of the examination."

Section 1(1)(b) states: "If an applicant fails to successfully complete the appropriate
examination within one (1) year from the date of the first notice of examination, the application shall be void." And
Section 1(5) states: "A passing score on an examination shall be valid for three (3) years."

## When does the license expire?

Section 3(3) says the initial license "shall expire on the final day of the applicant's birth month."

## Is continuing education required to renew?

Yes. Section 4(1) of 815 KAR 20:030 lists, for a journeyman renewal, "Proof of completing the continuing education
requirements established in 815 KAR 2:010".

815 KAR 2:010, "Continuing education requirements", Section 2(1), requires licensees not listed in its Section 2(2)
(journeyman plumbers are not listed there) to "provide proof of at least six (6) hours of approved continuing education
prior to license renewal." Section 1(2) says the hours are obtained "during the twelve (12) months prior to renewal".
Other points from the same regulation:

- Section 2(6): "Each master plumber's and journeyman plumber's continuing education courses shall relate to one (1)"
  or more of business, job safety, "The Kentucky State Plumbing Code", or subject matter "directly related to the
  plumbing trade."
- Section 1(5): "More than two (2) hours of safety practices and procedures per twelve (12) month period shall not be
  accepted".
- Section 1(3): extra hours may be carried "into the two (2) successive educational years", and "Carried forward credit
  hours shall be limited to a total of twelve (12) hours."
- Section 1(4): hours "shall not be required for a licensee's first renewal if the initial license was issued within
  twelve (12) months" of that renewal.
