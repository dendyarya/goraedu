This explainer covers **New Hampshire only**. It answers one practical question from New Hampshire's own administrative
rules for plumbing licensing (chapter Mec 300, published on the New Hampshire General Court's site): what does a person
need for a first **journeyman plumber** license? It describes the rules as published on the date above. Nothing here
describes a rule change, and nothing here applies to any other state.

## What are the two ways to qualify?

Mec 305.01, titled "Eligibility Requirements for Initial Individual Plumbing Licensure", sets out the journeyman
conditions in paragraph (b). Its lead-in reads: "A person shall be eligible for initial licensure as a journeyman
plumber if they meet the conditions of either (b)(1)-(3) or (4) below:".

So the rule groups its conditions into two sets: subparagraphs (1) to (3) together, or subparagraph (4) on its own.

## What does the first set, (b)(1) to (b)(3), require?

**Character and an apprenticeship certificate.** Under (b)(1), the applicant must be "of good character and professional
competence" as shown by the application form, any information submitted under Mec 304.04(a), and having "received an
apprenticeship completion certificate issued by the United States Department of Labor, or by the sponsoring plumber who
sponsored the apprenticeship".

**600 hours of education.** Under (b)(2), the applicant must "Have provided proof of successful completion of a minimum
of 600 hours of educational training" on a list of subjects. The list runs from "Plumbing systems drainage" and "Basic
and advanced plumbing theory" through "Backflow installation and protective devices", "System sizing and fixture
calculations" and "Adopted plumbing codes and standards, along with their amendments" to "New Hampshire statutory and
regulatory law governing plumbing".

**8,000 hours of work.** Under (b)(3), the applicant must "Have provided proof of completion of a minimum of 8,000
working hours of on-the-job experience in the trade relevant to plumbing as described in RSA 153:27, XIV". We could not
read RSA 153:27 itself today, so this post does not describe what that statute counts as relevant work.

## What does the second set, (b)(4), require?

Subparagraph (4) is for people already licensed elsewhere. The applicant must "Have completed all of the following":

- "Demonstrated proof of previous licensure as a journeyman plumber in any other state or jurisdiction responsible for
  the licensure of plumbers";
- "Taken the examination to become licensed as a journeyman plumber, which shall consist of multiple choice questions
  on, but not limited to, the topics listed in Mec 305.01(a)(3) above"; and
- "Passed the journeyman plumber examination by answering at least 70% of the questions correctly."

## Is there an exam for the first set?

Paragraphs (b)(1) to (b)(3) do not themselves mention an exam or a passing score. But Mec 304.04, "Required Supporting
Materials", lists what a journeyman application must include, and the last item is "The applicant's examination score".
The rules we read do not say what passing score, if any, applies to that score for applicants under (b)(1) to (b)(3), so
ask the board how the exam score is used for that group.

## What paperwork goes with a journeyman application?

Mec 304.04(a) applies "If the application is for a journeyman plumber license" and lists three items:

1. "A copy of the applicant's apprenticeship completion certificate or signed affidavit from the sponsor demonstrating
   apprenticeship completion";
2. "One signed letter written by the licensed plumber supervising, or directing the supervision of, the applicant's
   plumbing during the applicant's military service or apprenticeship", which must state that the writer supervised the
   work, "The writer's opinion that the applicant is competent to be a journeyman plumber", and the writer's name,
   address and telephone number; and
3. "The applicant's examination score".

## What do the rules say about the apprenticeship itself?

Mec 306.01 says "Licensed plumbers shall take on as apprentices only those individuals who" work under "a licensed NH
master plumber who shall supervise the work experience and provide for educational development under an apprenticeship
program meeting the requirements of the state apprenticeship council". On supervision it states: "The first 2 years of
supervision shall be direct supervision as defined in Mec 102.06" and "The remaining years of supervision shall be
supervised as defined in Mec 102.25". The apprentice must also "Hold a currently valid apprentice license issued by the
board." We did not read the Mec 102 definitions, so this post does not explain the two kinds of supervision.

## Is there an age limit, and how long does the license last?

Mec 302.02 states: "No individual fuel gas fitting or plumbing license shall be granted to any person less than 18 years
of age, and no apprentice license or trainee license shall be granted to any person less than 16 years of age." Mec
302.03 states: "An initial individual license shall be valid for 2 years." This post does not cover renewal or
continuing education.
