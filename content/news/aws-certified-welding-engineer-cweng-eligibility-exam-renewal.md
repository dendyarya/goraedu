The Certified Welding Engineer (CWEng) credential is run by the American Welding Society (AWS). AWS is a private
certifying body, not a government agency, and this explainer uses only AWS's own CWEng program page for facts about its
own program. It answers one practical question for welding certification: who can apply for the CWEng, what the exam
covers, and how long the credential lasts? Nothing here describes a rule change. Whether an employer, code or state
requires a CWEng is outside this post, and a CWEng is not described by AWS as a state engineering license.

## What does the CWEng cover?

AWS's program page says earning the credential "signifies that you have demonstrated the requisite knowledge and skill in
welding engineering," covering design, materials, processes and quality control. The page states that the certification
is tied to an AWS specification: "A CWEng certification ensures the certificant meets all of the specification and
certification requirements found in:" AWS B5.16:2006, Specification for the Qualification of Welding Engineers.

## Who is eligible?

The page states: "To qualify for the CWEng certification, applicants must meet one of the following education and
experience combinations:"

| Education                                       | Welding-based work experience (minimum) |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Bachelor of Science or higher in engineering    | one year                                |
| Bachelor of Science in engineering technology   | two years                               |
| Other related Bachelor of Science degrees       | five years                              |
| Associate in Applied Science (A.A.S.) degree    | 10 years                                |
| High school diploma or equivalent               | 15 years                                |

On what counts, AWS says: "Relevant experience can be in areas such as design, manufacturing, fabrication, construction,
research and development, and training." The page also says that a State Professional Engineering License in Welding
Engineering, or a diploma from the International Institute of Welding (IIW) or the European Federation for Welding,
Joining and Cutting (EWF) programs, may receive special consideration in the qualification process. Its FAQ says
applicants provide documentation of their education, work history and references. The page does not say that any other
AWS certification is required first.

## What is the exam?

AWS states: "AWS conducts the four exams via computer-based testing (CBT) at more than 450 Prometric Exam Centers across
the globe." The four parts named on the page are:

1. **Fundamentals of Science and Mathematics:** "This section tests your understanding of foundational concepts in
   mathematics, physics, and chemistry relevant to welding engineering."
2. **Applied Science and Welding Fundamentals:** "Building on the first part, this section focuses on metallurgical
   principles, welding processes, and material behavior under different conditions."
3. **Welding-Related Disciplines Examination:** "A written examination that evaluates your knowledge of industry
   standards and your ability to interpret specifications and codes in practical applications." The page's FAQ calls it
   "An open-book exam evaluating knowledge of industry standards, codes, and practical specifications."
4. **Practical Welding Engineering Applications:** "This final section includes open-book problem-solving tasks to
   assess your real-world problem-solving skills in areas such as design, production, and quality control."

The page does not state the number of questions, the time allowed or the passing score. It states: "AWS does not offer
an exam preparation seminar." It links an AWS CWEng Examination User Guide and an exam references and editions guide.

## How long is it valid, and how is it renewed?

AWS states: "The CWEng credential is valid for five years." The page also says it stays valid "for an indefinite period
as long as it is renewed every five (5) years."

For renewal, AWS says CWEng holders "must demonstrate successful completion of continued education every five years,
totaling 80 contact hours or a combination of contact hours and continuing education units related to the functions
defined in AWS B5.16." Its FAQ adds: "Certified Welding Engineers must recertify every five years to maintain their
credential." Renewals are filed through AWS's certification application portal.
