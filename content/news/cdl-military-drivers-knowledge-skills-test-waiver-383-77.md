Does military truck or bus driving count toward a civilian Commercial Driver's License (CDL)? The federal answer is in 49 CFR 383.77, "Substitute for knowledge and driving skills tests for
drivers with military CMV experience." This explainer reads it on eCFR (current to November 20, 2026). Nothing here describes a rule change. The section was last amended by 83 FR 48975 (published September 28, 2018; effective November 27, 2018).

## Do military drivers need a CDL while they serve?

Not for military driving. 49 CFR 383.3(c) states: "Each State must exempt from the requirements of this part
individuals who operate CMVs for military purposes." The section lists who the exception covers, including active duty
personnel, reservists, National Guard members on active duty and active duty Coast Guard personnel. Section 383.77 deals
with what happens when those drivers apply for a civilian license.

## Is the waiver automatic?

No. Every waiver in 383.77 is written as a State option. For the knowledge test, "a State may waive" the requirement.
For the driving skills test, the waiver is "At the discretion of a State," and the State "shall impose conditions and
limitations" on who may use it.

## Which tests can a State waive?

The section has three parts:

- **(a) Knowledge test** for a commercial learner's permit (CLP) or CDL, for "current or former military service
  members, as defined in § 383.5".
- **(b) Driving skills test** (CDL only), for "a CMV driver with military CMV experience who is currently licensed at the time of
  his/her application for a CDL". The skills test is "substituted with an applicant's driving record in combination
  with certain driving experience."
- **(c) Endorsement tests**, for a CLP or CDL: the skills test and specialized knowledge test for the passenger (P) endorsement, and the
  knowledge tests for the tank vehicle (N) and hazardous materials (H) endorsements. For H, the section adds: "States
  must continue to meet the requirements for a hazardous materials endorsement in subpart I of this part." The waiver
  covers only the H knowledge test.

## What must you show for the knowledge test waiver?

Under 383.77(a)(2), the applicant "must certify and provide evidence" that, "during the 1-year period immediately
prior to the application," he or she was regularly employed and designated in one of eight listed jobs: Motor
Transport Operator (88M), PATRIOT Launching Station Operator (14T) and Fueler (92F) in the Army; Vehicle Operator
(2T1), Fueler (2F0) and Pavement and Construction Equipment Operator (3E2) in the Air Force; Motor Vehicle Operator
(3531) in the Marine Corps; and Equipment Operator (E.O.) in the Navy. The applicant must also be operating, or have
operated immediately before separation, "a vehicle representative of the CMV type" he or she expects to operate after
leaving the military.

## What must you show for the skills test waiver?

The State's conditions "must require at least the following," so a State may add more. Under 383.77(b)(2)(i), the
applicant must provide evidence and certify that he or she:

- "Is regularly employed or was regularly employed within the last year in a military position requiring operation of
  a CMV";
- "Was exempted from the CDL requirements in § 383.3(c)"; and
- Was operating "a vehicle representative of the CMV type the driver applicant operates or expects to operate, for at
  least the 2 years immediately preceding separation from the military."

## What driving record rules apply?

Each waiver also requires a clean record over a set period: 1 year before the application for the knowledge test and
endorsement waivers, and the "2-year period immediately prior to applying for a CDL" for the skills test waiver. In
that period the applicant must not have (for the knowledge test and endorsement waivers the applicant must certify and
provide evidence of this; for the skills test waiver, (b)(2)(ii) requires the applicant to certify it):

- held more than one civilian license at the same time (in addition to a military license);
- had any license suspended, revoked or cancelled;
- had any conviction, in any type of motor vehicle, for the disqualifying offenses in 383.51(b);
- had more than one conviction, in any type of motor vehicle, for the serious traffic violations in 383.51(c); or
- had any conviction for a violation of military, State or local traffic control law (other than a parking violation)
  arising in connection with a crash, or any record of a crash in which he or she was at fault.

For the endorsement waivers, 383.77(c)(4)(i) also requires that during the same 1-year period the applicant is or was
regularly employed in a military position requiring operation of a passenger CMV (for P), operation of a tank vehicle
(for N) or transportation of hazardous materials (for H). The job list in (a) does not apply to these waivers.

## What this means for test study

383.77 lets a State waive tests; it does not change what the tests cover. The CDL rules in Part 383 are summarized in our [CDL standards study guide](/cdl/cdl-standards/study-guide/), with a
[practice test](/cdl/cdl-standards/practice-test/). The practice questions on goraedu are not the State knowledge test.
Confirm whether your State offers these waivers, and what proof it accepts, with your State's driver licensing agency.
