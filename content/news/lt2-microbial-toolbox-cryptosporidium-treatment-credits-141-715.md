A plant placed in a higher Cryptosporidium bin has to add treatment, and EPA's rules call the menu of ways to earn that
treatment the "microbial toolbox." This explainer answers one practical question: which toolbox options are there, and
how much Cryptosporidium credit does each one give? It reads 40 CFR 141.715 in the eCFR (text current to November 24,
2026). It is an explainer, not a rule change, and it covers only the federal text. How a plant is placed in a bin is in
our earlier post on [Cryptosporidium bins](/news/lt2-cryptosporidium-bin-classification-filtered-systems-141-710-141-711/).

## How do systems use the toolbox?

Section 141.715 is in Subpart W of Part 141 ("Enhanced Treatment for Cryptosporidium"). Paragraph (a)(1) states:
"Systems receive the treatment credits listed in the table in paragraph (b) of this section by meeting the conditions
for microbial toolbox options described in §§ 141.716 through 141.720. Systems apply these treatment credits to meet
the treatment requirements in § 141.711 or § 141.712, as applicable."

So the table is a summary. The detailed conditions for each option are in 141.716 to 141.720, which this post does not
cover.

## Can unfiltered systems use every option?

No. Under 141.715(a)(2), "Unfiltered systems are eligible for treatment credits for the microbial toolbox options
described in § 141.720 only." Section 141.720 holds the inactivation options (chlorine dioxide, ozone and UV in the
table below).

## What credit does each option earn?

The table in 141.715(b) groups sixteen options into five families. In short form (the table's own wording is longer and
lists more conditions):

| Option                                           | Credit stated in the table                                                       | Details in |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| (1) Watershed control program                    | 0.5-log; unfiltered systems not eligible                                         | 141.716(a) |
| (2) Alternative source/intake management         | "No prescribed credit"                                                           | 141.716(b) |
| (3) Presedimentation basin with coagulation      | 0.5-log in a month with a monthly mean turbidity reduction of 0.5-log or greater, or alternative State-approved performance criteria | 141.717(a) |
| (4) Two-stage lime softening                     | 0.5-log                                                                          | 141.717(b) |
| (5) Bank filtration                              | 0.5-log for a 25-foot setback; 1.0-log for a 50-foot setback (conditions apply, see below) | 141.717(c) |
| (6) Combined filter performance                  | 0.5-log                                                                          | 141.718(a) |
| (7) Individual filter performance                | 0.5-log, in addition to the combined filter credit                               | 141.718(b) |
| (8) Demonstration of performance                 | Credit based on a demonstration to the State with a State-approved protocol | 141.718(c) |
| (9) Bag or cartridge filters (individual)        | Up to 2-log                                                                      | 141.719(a) |
| (10) Bag or cartridge filters (in series)        | Up to 2.5-log                                                                    | 141.719(a) |
| (11) Membrane filtration                         | Credit equal to removal shown in a challenge test, if direct integrity testing supports it | 141.719(b) |
| (12) Second stage filtration                     | 0.5-log                                                                          | 141.719(c) |
| (13) Slow sand filters                           | 2.5-log as a secondary step; 3.0-log as primary filtration                       | 141.719(d) |
| (14) Chlorine dioxide | Based on measured CT in relation to the CT table | 141.720(b) |
| (15) Ozone | Based on measured CT in relation to the CT table | 141.720(b) |
| (16) UV | Based on validated UV dose in relation to the UV dose table | 141.720(d) |

## Which conditions does the table attach?

Several rows carry conditions in the table itself. A few of them, quoted from 141.715(b):

- **Watershed control program:** credit is "for State-approved program comprising required elements, annual program
  status report to State, and regular watershed survey."
- **Presedimentation:** "To be eligible, basins must be operated continuously with coagulant addition and all plant
  flow must pass through basins."
- **Two-stage lime softening:** credit applies "where chemical addition and hardness precipitation occur in both
  stages. All plant flow must pass through both stages. Single-stage softening is credited as equivalent to
  conventional treatment."
- **Bank filtration:** "aquifer must be unconsolidated sand containing at least 10 percent fines; average turbidity in
  wells must be less than 1 NTU." The table adds: "Systems using wells followed by filtration when conducting source
  water monitoring must sample the well to determine bin classification and are not eligible for additional credit."
- **Combined filter performance:** "0.5-log credit for combined filter effluent turbidity less than or equal to 0.15
  NTU in at least 95 percent of measurements each month."
- **Individual filter performance:** the extra 0.5-log applies "if individual filter effluent turbidity is less than or
  equal to 0.15 NTU in at least 95 percent of samples each month in each filter and is never greater than 0.3 NTU in
  two consecutive measurements in any filter."
- **Bag or cartridge filters:** credit is "based on the removal efficiency demonstrated during challenge testing with a
  1.0-log factor of safety" for individual filters, and with "a 0.5-log factor of safety" for filters in series.
- **Second stage filtration:** credit is "for second separate granular media filtration stage if treatment train
  includes coagulation prior to first filter."
- **Slow sand filters:** "No prior chlorination for either option."
- **UV:** "reactor validation testing required to establish UV dose and associated operating conditions."

The toolbox ties together topics a drinking water operator already studies: turbidity, filtration and disinfection.
