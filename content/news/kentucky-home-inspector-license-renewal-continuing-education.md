This explainer covers **Kentucky only**, where home inspectors are licensed by the Kentucky Board of Home Inspectors.
It answers one practical question: what does a Kentucky home inspector need for license renewal, and how much
continuing education counts? It describes the board's regulations, 831 KAR 2:040 and 831 KAR 2:020 on the Kentucky
Legislature's site, and the board's renewal checklist, as checked on Nov 21, 2026. Nothing here describes a rule
change, and nothing here applies to any other state.

## How many continuing education hours are required?

Section 11 of 831 KAR 2:040, headed "Continuing Education for Licensees.", sets two amounts, depending on the year of
renewal:

- "Each licensee who renews a license in an odd year shall have at least fourteen (14) hours" "per license year."
- "Each licensee who renews a license during an even year shall have at least twenty-eight (28) hours" "during the
  license biennial period."

The section "shall apply only to those licensees who will have been licensed at least twelve (12) months at license
renewal". The pages used here do not say how a renewal year is set.

## Which topics must the hours cover?

The section states: "Satisfaction of the educational requirements in Section 11(2)(a) and (b) shall consist of a
minimum of:"

- "Three (3) hours in KRS 198B.700 to 198B.738 and 831 KAR Chapter 2;" (Kentucky's home inspector law and the board's
  regulations)
- "Three (3) hours in report writing;"
- "Eight (8) hours in elective courses."

On electives, it adds: "Elective courses may be technical or non-technical in subject matter." Non-technical hours are
capped: for odd-year renewals, "non-technical courses shall be limited to three (3) hours of instruction for credit",
and for even-year renewals "non-technical courses shall be limited to six (6) hours of instruction for credit".

## Can the hours be taken online?

Not for the two required topics. The law-and-regulations hours and the report-writing hours "shall be completed
face-to-face." The section adds: "An online continuing education course shall not satisfy the continuing education
requirement for each respective category." For electives, Section 4(4)(b) says an online course "May satisfy the
continuing education requirements established in Section 11(3)(c) of this administrative regulation;".

## What else counts toward the hours?

- **The prelicensing course.** "An approved prelicensing course shall satisfy the initial fourteen (14) hour continuing
  education requirement."
- **Teaching.** "A maximum of three (3) hours per license year shall be awarded for teaching" "part of a home
  inspection continuing education course".
- **Board service.** "A maximum of three (3) hours per license year shall be awarded to a board member who is
  licensed and who has attended not less than eighty (80) percent of the board meetings each license year."

One more limit: "A licensee shall not receive credit for the same continuing education course during
a licensure period."

## What else does renewal ask for?

Section 5 of 831 KAR 2:020, "Renewal of Licenses.", requires a valid and current license and, besides a fee, lists:

- "Satisfy the continuing education requirements established by 831 KAR 2:040;"
- "Submit a completed Application for Licensure Renewal, Reinstatement or Reactivation, form KBHI-2", with "A
  certificate of completion for continuing education;" and "A certification of liability insurance showing continuous
  coverage;"
- "A criminal background check performed by the Kentucky State Police completed within ninety (90) days" of the date
  the board receives the renewal application;
- "Submit a copy of a completed inspection report that has been compiled within the previous twelve (12) months"
  before renewal.

The board's checklist adds details, such as how the insured name must appear, and lists the minimum insurance
coverage.

## What if a license is not renewed?

The regulation's sentence opens: "If a licensee failed to submit an Application for Licensure Renewal, Reinstatement or
Reactivation, form KBHI-2, within 120 days" and, counting from the last day of the licensee's renewal month, ends:
"the license shall be terminated." Under Section 5(2)(e), a licensee who failed to submit a timely form KBHI-2 "may
seek reinstatement upon a showing of good cause," "which shall include circumstances beyond the control of the
licensee."

## What about an inactive license?

Under 831 KAR 2:020, "A licensee in inactive status shall not engage in home inspection activities within the
Commonwealth of Kentucky." and "shall not be required to maintain the insurance coverage required by KRS
198B.712(3)(d)." For continuing education on return to active status, Section 12 of 831 KAR 2:040 asks for "Fourteen (14) hours of continuing
education consistent with Sections 11 (3) and 11 (8)", "for each year that the license has been inactive", or "A board approved sixty-four (64) hour
prelicensing training course." Section 7 of 831 KAR 2:020 adds that a license "that has been inactive for a period of five (5) years from the date of board
action" "shall be considered terminated."
