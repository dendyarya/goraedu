The Certified Welding Sales Representative (CWSR) credential is run by the American Welding Society (AWS). AWS is a
private certifying body, not a government agency, and this explainer uses only AWS's own CWSR program page for facts
about its own program. It answers one practical question for welding certification: who can become a CWSR, what the
exam involves, and what it takes to keep the credential? Nothing here describes a rule change. Whether an employer
asks for a CWSR is outside this post.

## What is a CWSR?

AWS's program page describes the people it certifies this way: "CWSRs work with manufacturers, OEMs, distributors,
fabrication shops, and industrial customers to recommend welding equipment, consumables, cutting systems, and related
services based on production needs."

The page ties the credential to two AWS documents: "AWS QC14 - Specification for the Certification of Welding Sales
Representatives" and "AWS B5.14 - Specification for the Qualification of Welding Sales Representatives". This post did
not read those two documents and relies only on AWS's program page.

## What education does AWS ask for?

The program page states: "Education: A high school diploma or equivalent is required."

## How much experience do you need?

The program page states: "Applicants should have at least five years of professional experience related to the sales
of welding and cutting equipment, supplies, and other associated services."

There is a shorter route. The same page states: "Alternatively, candidates with less than five years of experience can
qualify with at least two years of verifiable experience combined with a certificate of completion for training in
welding and cutting processes, as well as filler metals." The page does not name which training programs count, so
check with AWS before relying on a particular certificate.

## What does the exam involve?

AWS states: "Candidates for the CWSR certification must take a two-hour, closed-book exam consisting of 60 multiple
choice questions." It adds: "The CWSR exam is a computer-based test (CBT) administered through Prometric testing
centers located throughout the United States and internationally."

On scoring, the page states: "To pass the CWSR exam, candidates must score at least 70%."

The page says "The exam covers subjects specified in AWS B5.14," and lists technical welding and cutting subjects
such as "arc welding processes", "safety measures" and "AWS filler metal classifications". For anyone expecting a sales
test, the page's FAQ asks "Is the CWSR exam focused on sales techniques?" and answers: "No. This exam does not assess
sales techniques or closing skills." goraedu does not publish CWSR practice questions; the exam subjects and reference
list are on AWS's page.

## How long is the CWSR valid?

AWS's program page states: "The CWSR certification is valid for three years from the effective certification date."

It also states: "To remain active, certification and credential holders must complete the AWS renewal process before
their certifications or credentials expire and remain involved in welding sales or related industry activities."

## What happens at renewal and at nine years?

The page has a renewal section and a recertification section, and on the date checked both carry the same text. That
text begins: "The Certified Welding Sales Representative (CWSR) recertification comes into play after two consecutive
renewals, marking nine years of continuous certification."

It then states: "To retain their CWSR credentials, certificate holders must comply with the following:"

- "They need to submit evidence demonstrating continued involvement in the welding sales representative occupation."
- "They are obligated to pass the CWSR examination."

The exam can be replaced by continuing education. The page states: "However, this requirement can be bypassed if they
can provide proof of continuous education equivalent to 80 Professional Development Hours (PDHs) during the nine-year
certification period, with 20 PDHs completed within the three years preceding the nine-year recertification."

Because the same wording appears under both headings, the page does not spell out separately what the three-year
renewals in between require beyond completing "the AWS renewal process" and staying involved in welding sales. The
renewal application, or AWS itself, is the place to confirm exactly what you must show at each step.
