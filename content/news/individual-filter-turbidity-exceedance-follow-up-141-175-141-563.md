EPA's drinking water rules set turbidity limits for a filter plant's combined filtered water, and for many plants they
also require each filter to be watched on its own. This explainer answers one practical question from 40 CFR Part 141: when a single filter's
turbidity runs high, what must the system report, and when does a self-assessment or an outside evaluation follow? It
does not describe a rule change.

## Which rules apply to your plant

Two sets of sections cover the same subject for different system sizes:

- **At least 10,000 people (Subpart P).** Section 141.170(a) says Subpart P applies to "subpart H systems serving at
  least 10,000 people, beginning January 1, 2002 unless otherwise specified in this subpart." Section 141.174(a) applies
  to such a system "that provides conventional filtration treatment or direct filtration."
- **Fewer than 10,000 people (Subpart T).** Section 141.560 applies "If your system is a subpart H system serving fewer
  than 10,000 people and utilizing conventional filtration or direct filtration."

## Monitoring: every filter, recorded every 15 minutes

Section 141.174(a) requires "continuous monitoring of turbidity for each individual filter using an approved method in §
141.74(a)" and calibration of turbidimeters "using the procedure specified by the manufacturer." It adds: "Systems must
record the results of individual filter monitoring every 15 minutes." Section 141.560(c) sets a similar rule for smaller
systems: "Results of turbidity monitoring must be recorded at least every 15 minutes."

Smaller systems with few filters have an option in 141.562: "if your system only consists of two or fewer filters, you
may conduct continuous monitoring of combined filter effluent turbidity in lieu of individual filter effluent turbidity
monitoring."

## If the turbidimeter fails

- **Subpart P:** "the system must conduct grab sampling every four hours in lieu of continuous monitoring, but for no
  more than five working days following the failure of the equipment" (141.174(b)).
- **Subpart T:** grab sampling every four hours "until the turbidimeter is back on-line. Your system has 14 days to
  resume continuous monitoring before a violation is incurred" (141.561).

## What triggers action, by system size

All triggers count readings in pairs: two consecutive measurements taken 15 minutes apart.

| Trigger at an individual filter                 | At least 10,000 people (141.175(b))                                                                                                                                                         | Fewer than 10,000 people (141.563, 141.570)                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Above 1.0 NTU                                   | Report filter number, turbidity and date(s); within 7 days either produce a filter profile and report that it was produced, or report the obvious reason for the exceedance                 | Report filter number(s), date(s), turbidity value(s) and the cause (if known) by the 10th of the following month                                                                               |
| Above 0.5 NTU at the end of the first four hours of continuous operation after the filter is backwashed or otherwise taken offline | Same report, and the same filter profile or obvious-reason step within 7 days                                                                                       | No matching trigger in 141.563                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Above 1.0 NTU in each of three consecutive months | Filter self-assessment within 14 days of the exceedance, and report that it was conducted                                                                                                  | Filter self-assessment within 14 days of the third straight month's exceedance, unless a CPE under 141.563(c) was required                                                                    |
| Above 2.0 NTU in each of two consecutive months | Arrange a comprehensive performance evaluation (CPE) by the State or a State-approved third party no later than 30 days after the exceedance; completed and submitted to the State within 90 days | Arrange a CPE no later than 60 days after the second straight month's exceedance; completed and submitted within 120 days. No new CPE if one was completed within the prior 12 months or a joint CTA project is under way |

For systems with two filters that monitor CFE under 141.562, the 141.563 triggers apply to the CFE reading, and a
self-assessment covers both filters.

For the filter profile under 141.175(b)(1) and (2), the rule's own words are that the system must produce one "within 7
days of the exceedance (if the system is not able to identify an obvious reason for the abnormal filter performance)."

## What a self-assessment must include

Both 141.175(b)(3) and 141.563(b) list the same minimum parts: "assessment of filter performance; development of a filter
profile; identification and prioritization of factors limiting filter performance; assessment of the applicability of
corrections; and preparation of a filter self-assessment report."

## Monthly reports and records

- **Subpart P:** within 10 days after the end of each month, report that individual filter monitoring was done, and report results only if a trigger in
  (b)(1) to (4) occurred. Results must be kept "for at least three years" (141.175(b)).
- **Subpart T:** by the 10th of the following month, report "That your system conducted individual filter turbidity
  monitoring during the month" (141.570(b)(1)); a completed CPE report is due "Within 120 days after the CPE was
  triggered" (141.570(b)(5)). Results of individual filter monitoring are kept "At least 3 years" (141.571(a)).

## Lime softening plants

Both 141.175(b) and 141.564 let lime softening systems apply to the State for alternative exceedance levels if they can show, in
141.564's words, "that higher turbidity levels are due to lime carryover only, and not due to degraded filter
performance."
