For a Commercial Driver's License (CDL), this explainer looks at one question:
while you hold only a commercial learner's permit (CLP), can you drive with passengers on board? The federal rule is
49 CFR 383.25, "Commercial learner's permit (CLP)", read here with 383.93 (endorsements), 383.73(a) (what the State
does before it issues a CLP) and the definitions in 383.5. The text is the eCFR as it stood on November 19, 2026, the date it was last brought up to date. Nothing here describes a rule change.

## What does a CLP allow?

383.5 defines a CLP as a permit that, "when carried with a valid driver's license issued by the same State or
jurisdiction, authorizes the individual to operate a class of a commercial motor vehicle when accompanied by a holder
of a valid CDL for purposes of behind-the-wheel training."

383.25(a) says a CLP "is considered a valid CDL for purposes of behind-the-wheel training on public roads or
highways, if all of the following minimum conditions are met". One of them, in 383.25(a)(1), is that the CLP holder
"is at all times accompanied by the holder of a valid CDL who has the proper CDL group and endorsement(s) necessary
to operate the CMV and is otherwise authorized to operate the CMV for that trip." The limits below are further
conditions in the same list.

## Which endorsements can go on a CLP?

Only three. 383.93(a)(2) states: "The only endorsements allowed on a CLP are the following: (i) Passenger (P); (ii)
School bus (S); and (iii) Tank vehicle (N)." 383.25(a)(5)(iv) adds: "All other Federal endorsements are prohibited on
a CLP."

Each of the three needs its knowledge test first. 383.25(a)(5) states that a CLP holder with a P endorsement "must
have taken and passed the P endorsement knowledge test", and says the same for the S and N endorsements. On the State
side, 383.73(a)(4) tells the State to "Allow only a group-specific passenger (P) and school bus (S) endorsement and
tank vehicle (N) endorsement on a CLP, provided the applicant has taken and passed the knowledge test for the
specified endorsement." 383.25(a)(5)(i) adds, for the P endorsement only: "The P endorsement must be class specific."

## Can a CLP holder with a P endorsement carry passengers?

Only certain people. 383.25(a)(5)(i) states: "A CLP holder with a P endorsement is prohibited from operating a CMV
carrying passengers, other than Federal/State auditors and inspectors, test examiners, other trainees, and the CDL
holder accompanying the CLP holder as prescribed by paragraph (a)(1) of this section."

No one else is listed; members of the public are not among them. In a passenger vehicle, 383.25(a)(1) says the accompanying CDL
holder must be "directly behind or in the first row behind the driver and must have the CLP holder under observation
and direct supervision."

## What about a school bus?

The S endorsement has the same list. 383.25(a)(5)(ii) states: "A CLP holder with an S endorsement is prohibited from
operating a school bus with passengers other than Federal/State auditors and inspectors, test examiners, other
trainees, and the CDL holder accompanying the CLP holder as prescribed by paragraph (a)(1) of this section."

383.5 defines a school bus as "a CMV used to transport pre-primary, primary, or secondary school students from home
to school, from school to home, or to and from school-sponsored events" and adds that "School bus does not include
operations of a for-hire motor carrier." Students are not on the list in 383.25(a)(5)(ii).

## And a tank vehicle or hazardous materials?

For N, 383.25(a)(5)(iii) states: "A CLP holder with an N endorsement may only operate an empty tank vehicle and is
prohibited from operating any tank vehicle that previously contained hazardous materials that has not been purged of
any residue."

For hazardous materials, 383.25(a)(6) sets the condition that "The CLP holder does not operate a commercial motor
vehicle transporting hazardous materials as defined in § 383.5." The hazardous materials endorsement is not one of the three allowed on a CLP (383.93(a)(2) and 383.25(a)(5)(iv)).

## Is the CLP endorsement the same as the CDL endorsement?

No. For the CDL itself, 383.93(c) requires "a knowledge and a skills test" for the passenger and the school bus
endorsements, and "a knowledge test" for tank vehicle. For the CLP endorsements, 383.25(a)(5) and 383.73(a)(4) name only the knowledge test. 383.25(d) states that "The issuance of a CLP is a precondition to the initial issuance of a CDL", and
383.25(e) states that the CLP holder "is not eligible to take the CDL skills test in the first 14 days after
initial issuance of the CLP."

## What this means for study

On a CLP, the passenger and school bus endorsements limit who may ride to the people listed in 383.25(a)(5)(i) and (ii), the tank vehicle endorsement allows only an empty tank vehicle, and hazardous materials are not allowed. Your
State's own rules and procedures still apply. Our [CDL standards study guide](/cdl/cdl-standards/study-guide/) quotes
the other CLP rules in 383.25 (accompaniment, age, validity and the 14-day wait) and lists the P and S passenger limits
as not covered, which is what this post adds. You can also try the
[CDL standards practice test](/cdl/cdl-standards/practice-test/). The practice questions on goraedu are not the State
knowledge test.
