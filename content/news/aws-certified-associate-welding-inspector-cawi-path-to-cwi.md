This explainer covers the American Welding Society's Certified Associate Welding Inspector (CAWI) program, as AWS
described it on its own pages on the date checked. AWS is a private organization, not a government agency, and the
CAWI is an AWS credential, not a government license. goraedu is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
AWS. The practical question: if you do not yet have the years of experience the Certified Welding Inspector (CWI)
asks for, what does the CAWI offer, and how does it connect to the CWI? Nothing here describes a rule change.

## What kind of credential is the CAWI?

AWS presents it as an entry point, not a full inspector credential. Its CAWI page says: "A CAWI works under the
supervision of a CWI and is considered an entry-level certification." On who has the last word on the job, the page
adds: "A CAWI performs inspections alongside a SCWI or CWI, who determines final acceptance."

So a CAWI inspects, but the acceptance decision belongs to a Senior Certified Welding Inspector (SCWI) or a CWI.

## How much experience does the CAWI ask for?

The basic test matches the CWI's in shape: AWS says CAWI "candidates must pass a vision test and present documented
proof of a certain level of education and work experience." The amounts are lower. Put side by side with the CWI
page:

| Education (as each AWS page describes it)                     | CAWI minimum experience | CWI minimum experience |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| High school diploma or equivalent                             | 2 years                 | 5 years                |
| At least 8th grade (CWI page: "8th grade level of schooling") | 4 years                 | 9 years                |
| Less than 8th grade                                           | 6 years                 | 12 years               |

The CAWI page also lists two routes the table leaves out. Someone with at least an 8th grade education who has
completed one year of welding vocational training needs 3 years of experience; in AWS's words, those applicants,
"alongside one year of vocational training in welding, require 3 years of experience." And two years of post-high
school education in welding, engineering technology or physical sciences brings the minimum down to six months: "a
6-month work experience suffices."

The CWI page words each of its rows as "welding based work experience." Our earlier news post on CWI eligibility
covers that page in more detail.

## What do you send with the application?

Before you start the application, the CAWI page lists an "Employment Verification Form," a "Visual Acuity Exam
Form," an "Additional Qualifying Work Experience Form" and "Photo ID Requirements."

## What is the exam like?

The CAWI page lists three parts: Part A (Fundamentals), Part B (Practical) and Part C (Code Book). Parts A and C are
computer-based tests at Prometric centers. The CWI page refers to "The Part B Practical Exam for CAWI/CWI," naming the
two credentials together for that part.

On results, the CAWI page says: "Note, you will receive your Part B Exam score only after completing Parts A and C."
It adds: "Expect an email with exam scores about two weeks post completion."

## How does the score relate to the CWI?

The CAWI page states: "To progress to CWI, candidates need a minimum score of
72% on each part of the exam." The CWI page uses the same figure, saying candidates "must achieve a minimum score of
72% on each exam part to obtain the CWI credential."

The CAWI page we read does not give a separate passing score for the CAWI itself. We do not guess one.

## How long does a CAWI last?

AWS says the CAWI "is valid for three (3) years and cannot be renewed or recertified." For more
on that point, the page points readers to the program standard: "For additional details on the CAWI renewal or
recertification, please refer to" the AWS specification QC1:2016-AMD1.

## How do you move from CAWI to CWI?

AWS calls the credential "a steppingstone for the next level as a CWI." The page says that "As candidates gain
additional industry experience, they may qualify to take the CWI exam" (the sentence goes on to mention more advanced
roles). The CWI experience minimums in the table above are the ones to compare against.

The CWI page's fee tables include an item named "Upgrade CAWI to CWI." We do not repeat fees here, because they can
change.

## What the pages do not answer

Neither page we read says whether a CAWI holder must retake any exam parts to become a CWI, or how the three-year
validity interacts with the CWI application. Those details may be in QC1:2016-AMD1 or with AWS directly; we did not
find them on the pages listed below.
