The FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse is the database covered by Subpart G of 49 CFR Part 382, for commercial
driver's license (CDL) holders. This explainer answers one practical question: what can a driver do if information about
them in the Clearinghouse is wrong? The answer is in 49 CFR 382.717, "Procedures for correcting certain information in
the database", read in the eCFR (text current to November 24, 2026), with two neighboring sections on notices and
access. It is an explainer, not a rule change.

## How does a driver find out what is in the Clearinghouse?

Under 49 CFR 382.709, "A driver may review information in the Clearinghouse about himself or herself, except as
otherwise restricted by law or regulation." The same section adds: "A driver must register with the Clearinghouse
before accessing his or her information."

Section 382.707 also requires FMCSA to tell the driver about changes: "FMCSA must notify a driver when information
concerning that driver has been added to, revised, or removed from the Clearinghouse." FMCSA must also notify the driver
when information "has been released from the Clearinghouse to an employer and specify the reason for the release."
Notices go "by letter sent by U.S. Mail to the address on record with the State Driver Licensing Agency that issued the
driver's commercial driver's license", unless the driver gives the Clearinghouse "an alternative means or address for
notification, including electronic mail."

## What kind of error can a petition fix?

The petition process is narrow. Section 382.717(a)(1) states that petitioners "may request only that administrative
errors be corrected (e.g., errors in data entry or a duplicate report of a positive test result); petitioners may not
contest the accuracy of test results, test refusals, or other violation information, under this section."

Paragraph (a)(2) lists three exceptions. A petitioner may ask FMCSA to:

- add "documentary evidence of a non-conviction" to an employer's report of actual knowledge that the driver received a
  traffic citation for driving a commercial motor vehicle under the influence of alcohol or controlled substances, "if
  the citation did not result in a conviction" (the rule gives "conviction" the same meaning as in 49 CFR part 383);
- remove an employer's report of actual knowledge (other than the citation case above) "if that report does not comply
  with the reporting requirements in § 382.705(b)(5)"; and
- remove an employer's report of a violation under 49 CFR 40.261(a)(1) or 40.191(a)(1) "if that report does not comply
  with the reporting requirements in § 382.705(b)(3)."

## Who can file, and what goes in the petition?

Under 382.717(b), "Any driver or authorized representative of the driver may submit a petition to the FMCSA contesting
the accuracy of information in the Clearinghouse." The petition must include:

1. "The petitioner's name, address, telephone number, and CDL number and State of issuance;"
2. a "Detailed description of the basis for the allegation that the information is not accurate; and"
3. "Evidence supporting the allegation that the information is not accurate."

The rule warns: "Failure to submit evidence is cause for dismissing the petition."

Paragraph (c) says the petition may be submitted "electronically through the Clearinghouse or in writing" to FMCSA, ATTN:
Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse Petition for Review, at the Washington, DC address given in the section.

## How long does FMCSA take to decide?

- **Standard petition:** "Within 45 days of receiving a complete petition, FMCSA will inform the driver in writing of
  its decision to remove, retain, or correct the information in the database and provide the basis for the decision"
  (382.717(d)).
- **Expedited treatment:** a driver may ask for it if the inaccuracy or the employer report "is currently preventing him
  or her from performing safety-sensitive functions" (382.717(e)(1)). The request can be in the original petition or a
  separate document. "If FMCSA grants expedited treatment, it will subsequently inform the driver of its decision in
  writing within 14 days of receipt of a complete petition" (382.717(e)(2)).

## What if the driver disagrees with the decision?

Under 382.717(f), a driver who believes a decision "was in error" may ask FMCSA for an administrative review. The request
"must prominently state at the top of the document: 'Administrative Review of Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse Decision'"
and can be sent through the Clearinghouse or in writing. It "must explain the error he or she believes FMCSA committed
and provide information and/or documents to support his or her argument." FMCSA "will complete its administrative review
no later than 30 days after receiving the driver's request for review", and that decision "will constitute the final
Agency action."

## Are employers told about a correction?

Yes. Under 382.717(g), when information is corrected or removed under this section or under 49 CFR part 10, "FMCSA will
notify any employer that accessed the incorrect information that a correction or removal was made."
