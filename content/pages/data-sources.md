| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |

Works created by U.S. federal government employees as part of their official duties are not subject to copyright
in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105). We still link to each source and show the date we checked it, so you can
read the original.

## What we do not use

- Text, questions or page structure from other prep sites, books or courses.
- Real exam questions from any certifying organization.
- Standards text that is protected by copyright. Where a rule refers to an industry standard, we name it and link to
  the rule rather than reproduce the standard.

## Trademarks

Certification and organization names belong to their owners and are used only to identify the exam or source we
describe. goraedu is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. EPA or any certifying organization, and we do
not use any agency's logo or seal.

## Our content

The text, questions, explanations and design of goraedu are our own work. You may link to any page. Please
[contact us](/contact/) before reusing our questions or text elsewhere.
