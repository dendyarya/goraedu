## Sources in use

| Source                                                                 | Publisher                                                       | Use on goraedu                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/) | Office of the Federal Register and Government Publishing Office | Rule text behind questions and study pages, starting with 40 CFR Part 82, Subpart F for EPA 608 |
| [Section 608 pages on EPA.gov](https://www.epa.gov/section608)         | U.S. Environmental Protection Agency                            | EPA 608 certification requirements, test topics, explanations                                   |
| [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/)                   | Office of the Federal Register                                  | Proposed and final rules for news posts                                                         |

Each new certification adds its own sources to this table when its first pages are published.

Works created by U.S. federal government employees as part of their official duties are not subject to copyright
in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105). We still link to each source and show the date we checked it, so you can
read the original.

State manuals and private certifying bodies' materials can be protected by copyright. For those we link to the
official document and summarize it in our own words; we do not copy their text or questions.

## What we do not use

- Text, questions or page structure from other prep sites, books or courses.
- Real exam questions from any testing organization or certifying body.
- Standards text that is protected by copyright. Where a rule refers to an industry standard, we name it and link to
  the rule rather than reproduce the standard.

## Trademarks

Certification and organization names belong to their owners and are used only to identify the exam or source we
describe. goraedu is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency or certifying organization, and we do
not use any agency's or organization's logo or seal.

## Our content

The text, questions, explanations and design of goraedu are our own work. You may link to any page. Please
[contact us](/contact/) before reusing our questions or text elsewhere.
