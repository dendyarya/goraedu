**Effective date: November 5, 2026**

goraedu is built to work without collecting personal information. You do not need an account, and no question or
tool asks for your name or email address.

## What we collect

**Server and network logs.** Like nearly every website, our web server and our network provider record technical
information when a page is requested: IP address, date and time, the page requested, the referring page and your
browser type. We use these logs only to keep the site running and secure. Our own server logs are deleted after
14 days.

**Cloudflare.** The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes the same technical information to
protect the site from attacks and speed up delivery. Cloudflare's handling is described in its
[privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

**Email you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

## What we do not do

- We do not use advertising or analytics cookies at this time, and we do not show ads.
- We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.
- Practice answers and tool inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.

If we add analytics, advertising or an email newsletter in the future, we will update this policy before doing so,
describe the change here, and provide any choices required by law.

## Children

goraedu is intended for adults and older students preparing for a professional certification. It is not directed to
children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct
it, or to delete it. Because we keep very little, most requests concern email you sent us. Write to
contact@goraedu.com with "Privacy" in the subject line.

## Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
