Email: **contact@goraedu.com**

## Reporting an error

Please include:

1. The page address (URL), or the question you are writing about.
2. What you believe is wrong.
3. The official source that shows the correct information, if you have it.

We review every error report within 7 days and log fixes on the [corrections page](/corrections/).

## What we cannot help with

We are an independent education website, not a government agency, a testing company or a certifying organization.
We cannot issue, look up or replace certificates, cards or licenses, schedule exams, or give legal or compliance
advice for a specific job. For a certificate or card, contact the organization or agency that gave you the test.

## Privacy requests

To ask about personal information, write to the same address with "Privacy" in the subject line. See our
[privacy policy](/privacy/) for what we collect.
