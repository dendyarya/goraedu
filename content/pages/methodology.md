## Sources we use

We build every question, explanation and study page from primary U.S. government sources:

| Source                                                                                                            | What we use it for                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [eCFR: 40 CFR Part 82, Subpart F](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-82/subpart-F) | The Section 608 rules themselves: definitions, venting prohibition, recovery, evacuation, leak repair, technician certification |
| [EPA: Section 608 pages](https://www.epa.gov/section608)                                                          | EPA's explanations, test topics and certification requirements                                                                  |
| [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/)                                                              | New and proposed rules, for our news and updates                                                                                |

We do not use exam question banks, "dumps", other prep sites or forum posts as sources. Trade press can point us
to a change, but we only publish what the official document says.

## How we label information

| Label           | Meaning                                                                           |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Official data   | Taken from a U.S. government source, shown with a link and the date we checked it |
| Our calculation | A formula applied to official data, with the formula explained on the page        |
| Your input      | A value you entered in a tool and can change                                      |

## Dates on every page

- **Last checked** is the date we last compared the page with its sources.
- **Last updated** is the date we last changed the page.
- News posts show the date they were first published, and an updated date if we corrected them.

## Keeping pages current

- We re-check every page and every practice question against its sources at least once every 12 months.
- When EPA publishes a final rule that affects technicians, we update the affected pages and write a
  [news post](/news/) explaining the change.
- A proposed rule is never described as being in effect.
