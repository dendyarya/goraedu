## Independent education website

goraedu is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the U.S. Environmental Protection
Agency or any EPA-approved certifying organization. Certification and organization names are used only to identify
the exams and sources we describe.

## Not the official exam

Our practice questions are written by us from official sources. They are not questions from any real exam, and a
good score on goraedu does not guarantee that you will pass. Certification requires passing a test from an
[EPA-approved certification program](https://www.epa.gov/section608/section-608-technician-certification-0).

## Not legal or compliance advice

goraedu explains federal rules for study purposes. It does not tell you what a specific job, employer, state or
local authority requires. State and local rules, manufacturer instructions and workplace safety rules may add
requirements beyond the federal rules we cover.

## Rules change

EPA updates its regulations from time to time. We show the date each page was last checked, but the official text
in the [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-82/subpart-F) and on EPA.gov is
always the final word.

## Tools and calculators

Any calculator on goraedu is for learning. Do not use its results as the only basis for work on real equipment.
Follow the applicable rules, the equipment manufacturer's instructions and safe work practices.
