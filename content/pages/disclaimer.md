## Independent education website

goraedu is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government agency, testing
company or certifying organization, including the U.S. EPA. Certification and organization names are used only to
identify the exams and sources we describe.

## Not the official exam

Our practice questions are written by us from official sources. They are not questions from any real exam, and a
good score on goraedu does not guarantee that you will pass. Certification requires passing the official test from
the organization or agency that gives it.

## Not legal or compliance advice

goraedu explains rules for study purposes. It does not tell you what a specific job, employer, state or local
authority requires. State and local rules, manufacturer instructions and workplace safety rules may add requirements
beyond the rules we cover.

## Rules change

Agencies update their regulations from time to time. We show the date each page was last checked, but the official
text, such as the [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/) or the agency's own site, is always the final word.

## Tools and calculators

Any calculator on goraedu is for learning. Do not use its results as the only basis for real work. Follow the
applicable rules, the equipment manufacturer's instructions and safe work practices.
