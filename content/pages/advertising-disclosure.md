## Display ads

To keep the site free, goraedu shows display ads served by Google AdSense. Google chooses which ads appear; we do not
choose, endorse or check the advertisers, and an ad is not a recommendation from goraedu.

- Ads are kept separate from our content and are never placed inside a practice question, its answers or a tool's
  results.
- No advertiser pays to change a question, an answer, an explanation, a study guide or what the official rules say.
- How ads use cookies, and how to turn off personalized ads, is explained in our [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Affiliate links

goraedu contains no affiliate links. If we add any, they will be disclosed on the page where they appear, as the U.S.
Federal Trade Commission requires, and free and official options, including the agency's own resources, will still
be mentioned where they exist.

We will update this page and our [privacy policy](/privacy/) before adding any other advertising or affiliate program.
