goraedu helps people prepare for the certification exams that U.S. skilled trades require, from refrigerant
handling and commercial driving to workplace safety, electrical work and drone flight.

## What we do

We write practice questions, study guides and simple tools for each certification we cover. Every answer and every
fact links to the official source it comes from, usually the regulation itself in the Code of Federal Regulations,
the agency that writes the rule, or the Federal Register. Next to each source we show the date we last checked it.

We cover one certification at a time and finish it before starting the next. EPA Section 608 comes first. The full
list, and what is planned, is on the [certifications page](/certifications/).

## Who we are

goraedu is an independent education website. We are not contractors, instructors, a testing company or a
government agency, and we do not present ourselves as experts in any trade. What we offer is careful reading of the
official rules, explained in plain English, with the source always one click away so you can check it yourself.

goraedu is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency or certifying organization. Certification
names are used only to identify each exam. To get certified, you must pass the official test from the organization
or agency that gives it.

## How we keep it accurate

- We only write questions whose answers are stated clearly in an official document.
- We check every page against its sources at least once a year, and sooner when a rule changes.
- We review error reports within 7 days and log every fix on our [corrections page](/corrections/).

The details are on our [methodology](/methodology/) and [question writing standards](/question-writing-standards/)
pages.
