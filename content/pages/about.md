goraedu helps people prepare for the EPA Section 608 technician certification exam, the test that U.S.
technicians pass before they service or dispose of refrigeration and air-conditioning equipment.

## What we do

We write practice questions, study guides and simple tools for the topics EPA lists for the exam. Every answer and
every fact links to the official source it comes from: the Code of Federal Regulations (40 CFR Part 82,
Subpart F), EPA.gov, or the Federal Register. Next to each source we show the date we last checked it.

Our first subject is EPA Section 608. We plan to add other skilled trades certifications later, one subject at a
time.

## Who we are

goraedu is an independent education website. We are not HVAC contractors, instructors or an EPA-approved
certifying organization, and we do not present ourselves as experts. What we offer is careful reading of the
official rules, explained in plain English, with the source always one click away so you can check it yourself.

goraedu is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency or any certifying
organization. Certification names are used only to identify each exam. To get certified, you must pass a test
from an [EPA-approved certification program](https://www.epa.gov/section608/section-608-technician-certification-0).

## How we keep it accurate

- We only write questions whose answers are stated clearly in an official document.
- We check every page against its sources at least once a year, and sooner when EPA changes a rule.
- We review error reports within 7 days and log every fix on our [corrections page](/corrections/).

The details are on our [methodology](/methodology/) and [question writing standards](/question-writing-standards/)
pages.
