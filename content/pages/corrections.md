We want every answer on goraedu to match the official rules. When we get something wrong, we fix it and record the
fix here.

## How to report an error

Email the page address, what you think is wrong and, if you can, the official source that shows the correct
information. Our address is on the [contact page](/contact/). We review every report within 7 days.
